02 The product in development

The Android and iOS prototype brings together four ways for adults to practise everyday wellbeing:

  • AI Wellbeing Companion. Reflect through text chat or a timed voice conversation.
  • Affirmations. Read, listen, save favourites and add background sounds.
  • Guided Meditations. Browse and play narrated sessions.
  • Relaxing Sounds. Play ambient and nature sounds, use playlists or accompany an affirmation.

The UK is the first planned market. Screens show an internal build. Features, catalogue and availability may change during development. There is no confirmed public release date.

The app does not diagnose conditions, deliver psychotherapy or provide medical treatment. There is no emergency response service in the app. In an emergency, contact your local emergency services directly.
