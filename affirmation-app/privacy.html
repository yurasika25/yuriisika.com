01 Who to contact

AffirmationApp is an independent, founder-led project operated by Yurii Sika, an individual based in the United Kingdom. Yurii Sika is the data controller for personal information processed in operating the AffirmationApp website and waitlist, and for the internal app prototype under his responsibility. As controller, he determines the purposes and means of that processing. For privacy enquiries or requests to exercise your data protection rights, contact yurii.sika@outlook.com.

This notice covers the AffirmationApp pages, waitlist and enquiries. The mobile sections describe the internal Android and iOS prototype. Visiting these pages does not create an app account, submit a mood check-in or start an AI session.

02 Joining or leaving the waitlist

The form asks for your email address, your permission to receive updates and, optionally, your name. When you submit it, your browser sends those fields, the full page URL, browser user-agent string and a source label to Formspree. A hidden field helps identify automated submissions. Formspree also receives the network information needed to handle your request.

We use consent to send the launch emails you request. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing yurii.sika@outlook.com with “unsubscribe”. Withdrawal does not affect earlier lawful processing. You do not have to join the list to browse, and signing up does not reserve a purchase.

The proposed basis for using submission metadata and the spam check is our legitimate interest in protecting the form and diagnosing delivery problems. The founder needs to confirm that the data collected is necessary and proportionate.

03 Browsing and contacting us

When you open a page, the web host receives your request, including the requested address and your IP address, to deliver it. If you email us, your email provider and ours handle your address, message and any details you choose to include.

Our proposed legitimate interests are delivering and securing the site and answering messages that visitors send us. Please leave sensitive details out of ordinary email. We handle requests to exercise data rights to meet our legal obligations.

04 Optional analytics and your choice

Google Analytics is off by default on these three pages. Use Analytics preferences in the footer to accept or reject it. You can return there to change your mind. The waitlist has its own consent checkbox.

If you accept, the page requests Google’s measurement tag. Google receives connection and browser information and can measure page views and interactions using identifiers in first-party analytics cookies. The page supplies its address without the query string or fragment and disables Google signals and advertising personalisation in the tag configuration. We rely on consent for this optional measurement and its cookies.

We save your choice and its expiry in browser local storage for 180 days so that the pages can respect it. The tag is configured to set measurement cookies for up to 180 days from creation. Rejecting turns off further measurement on these pages and clears the measurement cookies set here. Withdrawing does not erase reports already received by Google. Clearing your browser data also clears your preference. Other sections of the portfolio have their own settings.

05 Accounts and everyday app features

The mobile app is an internal working prototype. Its account, AI and usage processing already matters for anyone invited to test. The founder still needs to document the legal basis for each of those purposes and give testers the corresponding notice. Its code uses Supabase for authentication and backend records. It includes Google sign-in, which exchanges an identity token to sign the user into the app. Signed-in features use an account identifier. The prototype also has a request to delete the authenticated account.

A person can select a mood, read or listen to an affirmation, save favourites, browse narrated meditations and play ambient audio or playlists. The code saves affirmation favourites against a user ID on the device and supports synchronising them. For signed-in listening sessions, it records the session type, start time, seconds listened and completion state. We have not confirmed whether an individual mood choice is retained or how long these records are kept.

06 What happens in an AI text conversation

The prototype sends your message to a Supabase Edge Function under your signed-in account. The current chat processor requires saved history. If you turn history off, it declines new messages. With history on, it stores conversation and message records linked to your account, including replies.

When the OpenAI integration is enabled, it sends the message and relevant conversation context to OpenAI to generate a reply. The request asks OpenAI not to store the response. This setting alone does not establish what operational logs the provider keeps or how its account-level data controls are configured.

The chat preferences include an age declaration, region and consent version. A declaration does not independently verify age. The prototype lets you request deletion of a conversation. That is separate from deleting your account. The handling of backups, logs and copies already processed by providers still needs verification.

07 What happens during a voice session

When you start a timed call and allow microphone access, the prototype uses Supabase to authorise the session and establish a real-time audio connection with OpenAI. Microphone audio is transmitted so the system can listen and respond. Use the session controls to mute the microphone or end the call.

The backend tracks the account, session identifier, provider call identifier, timing and session status to manage access, time allowances and disconnection. The app also uses audio-level readings to animate the voice interface. A session record or level reading is different from a recording of what you said.

Whether the provider generates or keeps transcripts, retains raw audio or uses any content for model improvement has not been established for this deployment. These points, and the applicable processing terms, require confirmation before wider use. Avoid sharing private information about other people.

08 Wellbeing information and younger visitors

A mood choice on its own need not reveal a health condition. A conversation about symptoms or a diagnosis, or an inference drawn from it, can reveal health information. That can be special-category data under UK data protection law.

Before processing such information the founder needs both a lawful basis and an applicable Article 9 condition. The ordinary waitlist consent and the app’s consent screen do not establish either for all app uses. The founder needs to assess risks to testers as well as future users and carry out a data protection impact assessment where the use is likely to pose a high risk.

The product is intended for adults. This website does not verify visitors’ ages. Before wider access the founder must assess whether under-18s are likely to use the service, and which Children’s Code safeguards apply. Contact us if a child’s details have been submitted.

09 Recipients, locations and retention

The website uses Formspree for submissions and Google for optional measurement. The app integrations described above are separate. Following an external link, for example to LinkedIn or GitHub, takes you to a different provider’s website.

Free, Premium and metered Voice Packs are proposed. This website collects no payment details and creates no subscription entitlements. Any future purchase processing will need a separate explanation before purchase.

Service providers may process information outside the UK. Hosting locations, overseas access, contracts and the mechanism for each restricted transfer still need to be confirmed. Contact us for the available details of recipients and safeguards.

The purpose sets the retention criterion. A waitlist entry is useful while you want launch updates. An enquiry is needed while we deal with it and any follow-up. A limited record of withdrawal may be needed to prevent further mailings. Specific retention and deletion arrangements for Formspree, mailboxes, analytics reports, app records, diagnostics, provider logs and backups require approval. Browser cookie lifetimes do not determine how long providers keep server records.

10 Your rights and complaints

You can ask to see your personal data, correct it, request erasure or restrict its use. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests and object at any time to direct marketing. Where automated processing relies on consent or contract, portability may let you receive or transfer information you supplied in a reusable format. The rights available depend on the circumstances.

Write to yurii.sika@outlook.com describing your request and the email or account it concerns. We may need enough information to verify your identity. Tell us if you are unhappy with the response. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
