Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Affirmation App (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our app and this website.

Effective date
02 March 2026
Contact
affirmspace@outlook.com

1. Information we collect

  • Waitlist / contact submissions: if you join the waitlist or contact us, we may collect your email address and optional name, plus technical metadata (e.g., page URL, user agent) to help prevent spam and troubleshoot.
  • App data (if applicable): the app may store preferences such as theme, typography, reminders, or favorites. (Exact behavior depends on the current app version.)
  • Analytics (optional): if analytics are enabled, we may collect anonymized or aggregated usage information to improve performance and user experience.

2. How we use information

  • To respond to your requests and provide support.
  • To notify you about launch updates or early access (waitlist).
  • To maintain and improve the app and website (performance, reliability, and UX).
  • To prevent abuse, spam, and fraudulent submissions.

3. Waitlist processing (Formspree)

We use a third-party form provider (Formspree) to process waitlist submissions on this website. When you submit the waitlist form, the information is sent to Formspree and then forwarded to us.

Please review Formspree’s policies for details about their processing and retention practices.

4. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only:

  • With service providers that help us operate the website/app (e.g., form handling, analytics), only as needed to provide the service.
  • If required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security.

5. Data retention

We keep waitlist submissions only as long as needed to communicate about the launch and early access, or as required for legal, security, or operational purposes. You can request deletion at any time.

6. Your choices

  • You can unsubscribe from waitlist emails at any time (when emails begin).
  • You can request access, correction, or deletion of your information by contacting us.

7. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. International users

If you access the website or app from outside the country where we operate, your information may be processed in different jurisdictions depending on the service providers used.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact: affirmspace@outlook.com
